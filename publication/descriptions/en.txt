🧹 Clean up Facebook Messenger faster with Messenger Cleaner.

Messenger Cleaner is a productivity-focused Chrome extension for bulk deleting Facebook Messenger conversations, archiving chats, and restoring archived conversations from a convenient side panel. Stop opening conversation menus one by one and organize an overloaded Messenger inbox with clear progress and safe controls.

✨ KEY FEATURES
• Bulk delete Messenger conversations
• Archive multiple Facebook chats
• Restore conversations from Archived chats
• Persistent side panel that follows tab and Messenger navigation
• Live progress, stop controls, and daily usage counter
• Safe pause when the Messenger tab is hidden
• Multiple processing speeds
• Support for popular Facebook interface languages

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
Conversation processing happens locally in your browser and within your existing Facebook session. Messenger Cleaner does not collect message contents, Facebook passwords, cookies, friend lists, or conversation data. No advertising or behavioral tracking is included.

🎁 FREE DAILY CLEANUP
Use up to 10 successful delete or archive actions per day. Restoring archived chats is free. Upgrade to unlimited access when you need larger Messenger inbox cleanups.

⚠️ IMPORTANT
Deleting a conversation can be permanent. Messenger Cleaner requires explicit confirmation before deletion. Facebook may change its interface, so keep the Messenger tab visible while an operation runs.
